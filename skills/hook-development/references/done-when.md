# Done when

- A `command` check: it is wired and a decision row appears under a real host session id, distinct from any direct probe; its row was seen to move against a deliberate break in a copy and to return after a plain `cp` restore, as `testing.md` § Break on purpose, and that proof is in the reply; a blocking check carries either an observe marker with a dated review or a recorded flip; and the registry row landed in the same change as the check.
- A `type: "prompt"` hook writes no row and has no rollout window — `references/rollout.md` § A model-judged hook cannot be instrumented says what it is done on.
- A retirement: the row is replaced by its retirement clause, the source is deleted or archived per `registry.md` § Retiring a check, the wiring and rollout table are cleared in the same change, and `hook-decisions --drift` lists the id as historical.
- An audit: the inventory reconciles at zero, or every survivor carries a recorded reason.
