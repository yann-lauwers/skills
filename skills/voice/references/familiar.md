- Friends, partner, family, close colleagues.
- Corpus: 3 082 of Yann's own WhatsApp messages to five correspondents.
- Very short: median 4–7 words; a fifth to two fifths of messages run 1–3 words; effectively nothing over 25 words (0–4%).
- Several messages in a row rather than one paragraph.
- Abbreviations, unmarked: `jsp` `pcq` `mtn` `tjrs` `bcp` `qque(s)` `ajd` `pe` `tqt` `fin` (= enfin) `att` `bah` `dsl` `poss` `thx` `tbh` `//`.
