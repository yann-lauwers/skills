# Source lane B — a figure outside the machine

- Subject → a real public figure, or a fictional one.
- Run /research against primary sources: their own words first — interviews · letters · transcripts · shooting scripts — then what others record about them.
- Quoted lines beat descriptions of their manner → write the persona from cadence, never from adjectives.
- Done → the Source row names the sources drawn from, every row the quoted lines answer is filled, and the empty rows and contradictions are listed for the grilling (`SKILL.md` § Grilling lane).
