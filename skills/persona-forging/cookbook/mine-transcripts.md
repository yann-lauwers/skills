# Source lane A — a person in the transcripts

- Subject → the owner, or anyone whose words this machine already holds.
- Mine the corpus; never ask what it already answers.
- Extract their typed turns.
- Count what they reach for.
- Pull the moments they corrected someone.
- Set their stated self-image beside their measured behaviour → every disagreement between the two goes to the grilling, which arbitrates it (`SKILL.md` § Grilling lane).
- Done → the Source row names the corpus drawn from, every row the evidence answers is filled, and the empty rows and contradictions are listed for the grilling.
